Questions

Is it a real vinyl record?

Yes. A real 12-inch upcycled record. Every record we sell already had a life before it got to you; we clean it up and set the artwork into the centre label. Nothing is pressed new for decor.

Can I play it?

It's sold to hang, not to play. These are real records, so the grooves are real — but we make no promise about what's on them or how it sounds, and the label carries our artwork rather than the original. Treat it as a wall piece.

Why upcycled and not new?

Because a record that already had a life is the whole point of the name. It also means no two are identical, and nothing new gets pressed just to hang on a wall.

How does a record hang?

Every record comes with a wall hook in the box. Put the hook up and hang the record on it.

What if it arrives damaged?

Email contact@pastforward.shop within 7 days of delivery with a photo, and we'll put it right. See returns.

Can you make one that isn't on the site?

Yes. That's what custom orders are for — tell us the artist and album and we'll make it.

How do I look after it?

Wipe with a dry cloth. Keep it out of direct sunlight — same as the original.
