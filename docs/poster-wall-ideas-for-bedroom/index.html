Poster wall ideas for your bedroom

Six layouts, with the sizes and spacing that make each one work.

1. One statement piece

A single A3 (29.7 × 42 cm) centred above the bed, its bottom edge 20 to 25 cm above the headboard. The simplest wall that still looks finished.

2. The row

Three A3s or three 10×10 prints in a line, 5 cm apart. Pick three albums from one artist or one era so the row reads as a set.

3. The grid

Four or nine 10×10 prints in a square, gaps kept identical. Album covers are square to begin with, so a grid of them looks deliberate.

4. The gallery mix

One A3 as the anchor, with A5s and 10×10s around it. Lay everything out on the floor first, then copy it to the wall with paper templates and removable putty.

5. The polaroid wall

Fifteen to twenty polaroid cards in a tight grid, or clipped to a string across the wall. The cheapest way to cover a lot of wall.

6. Records with posters

An upcycled record next to the A3 poster of the same album. The round shape breaks up a wall of rectangles.

Questions

How do I put posters up without damaging the wall?

Removable mounting putty or poster strips. Ordinary tape can lift paint when it comes off.

What size poster goes above a bed?

An A3 for a single bed, or two or three A3s in a row for a double. See the poster size guide.
