Questions

How do custom vinyl records and custom posters work?

For custom vinyl records, we take authentic vintage 12-inch vinyl records, restore their surface, and press high-definition custom album art directly onto the centre label, complete with a wall hook. For custom posters, we reproduce your requested album art or personal photo on 300 GSM matte art paper in A3, A5, 10×10, or polaroid sizes.

Are custom vinyl records playable on a turntable?

No. Like every record we make, our custom vinyls are designed to hang as wall decor, not to play on a turntable. The authentic vintage vinyl grooves are 100% real, but they are crafted and sold as aesthetic music wall decor.

Can I order custom posters or vinyl for Spotify playlists or unreleased music?

Yes. You can request any album, mixtape, personal playlist, single track, or personal photo. Put the title, artist, or playlist link in the form above and we will confirm the visual layout with you.

What paper and materials are used for custom posters and vinyls?

Custom vinyl records use authentic 12-inch reclaimed phonograph discs (⌀305mm) with printed centre labels and pre-installed hanging hooks. Custom posters use heavy 300 GSM archival matte artboard that prevents curling and reflections.

Where can I buy custom vinyl records and custom posters in India?

PastForward hand-crafts and delivers custom vinyl records and custom posters across India with free shipping and 7–10 day delivery.

Can you add custom text, anniversary dates, or personalized messages?

Yes. Mention your requested text, Spotify code, or date in the notes field. We will include it in the artwork preview when confirming your order.
