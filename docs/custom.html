How it works

Send the request above. We confirm the artwork with you before anything is made, then it's made and sent anywhere in India. Delivery is free, anywhere in India.

Questions

Can I get a song instead of a whole album?

Yes. Put the song in the album field and tell us in the notes.

Can you add text, like a name or a date?

Ask in the notes. We'll tell you what's possible when we confirm the artwork.

Does a custom record play?

No. Like every record we make, it's sold to hang, not to play.

Does custom cost extra?

No. A custom piece costs the same as that format in the catalogue.
