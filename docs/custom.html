Questions

How do custom vinyls work?

We take real 12-inch records that already had a life, clean them up, and put your album art on the centre label. Each one comes ready to hang, with a wall hook in the box.

Are custom vinyls playable?

No. Like every record we make, custom vinyls are made to hang, not to play. The grooves are real, but the record is sold as wall art.

Can I order a playlist or unreleased music?

Yes. Any album, mixtape, playlist or your own photo collage works. Put the details in the form above and we'll confirm the artwork with you.

Do you deliver everywhere in India?

Yes, to all 19,000+ pin codes, free, in 7–10 days.

Can I get a song instead of a whole album?

Yes. Put the song in the album field and tell us in the notes.

Can you add text, like a name or a date?

Ask in the notes. We'll tell you what's possible when we confirm the artwork.

Does custom cost extra?

No. A custom piece costs the same as that format in the catalogue: ₹999 for a 12-inch vinyl record.
