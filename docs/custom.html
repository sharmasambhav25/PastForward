Questions

How do custom vinyls work?

We take authentic vintage 12-inch vinyl records, restore their surface, and press high-definition custom album art directly onto the centre label. Each piece is crafted as ready-to-hang vinyl record wall decor with a damage-free mounting hook included in the box.

Are custom vinyls playable?

No. Like every record we make, our custom vinyls are designed to hang as wall decor, not to play on a turntable. The authentic vinyl grooves are real, but they are sold as visual wall art.

Can I order custom vinyls for custom Spotify playlists or unreleased music?

Yes. You can request any album, mixtape, personal playlist, or personal photo collage. Put the details in the form above and we will confirm the artwork.

Where can I buy vinyl record wall decor in India?

Can you add text, like a name or a date?
