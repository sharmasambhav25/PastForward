Made to order

Each piece is printed and put together for your order, so we can't take returns if you change your mind.

If it arrives damaged

If your order arrives damaged, or isn't what you ordered, email contact@pastforward.shop within 7 days of delivery. Include your order details and photos of the piece and the packaging. We'll send a replacement, or refund you if we can't.

About the records

Our records are upcycled: real vinyl that already had a life. Small surface marks on the record are part of that history and aren't counted as damage.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method.
