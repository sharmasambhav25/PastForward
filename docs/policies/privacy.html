Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

What we collect

Only what you give us. When you order: your name, delivery address, phone number and email, so we can deliver. When you use a form: what you type in it, such as your name, email, WhatsApp number, message, or a link to the artwork you want. When you join the list: your email.

What we use it for

To make and deliver your order, to reply to you, and, if you joined the list, to tell you about new drops. You can leave the list at any time.

Payments

Payments are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your card or bank details.

Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to get your order to you, such as our payment provider and delivery partner. We don't sell your data.

Your cart and cookies

Your cart is saved in your own browser so it's still there when you come back. This site uses the Meta Pixel to measure which ads and posts bring visitors and sales, and to show our ads to people who have visited. You can control this in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email contact@pastforward.shop.
