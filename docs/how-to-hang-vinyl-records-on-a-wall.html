How to hang vinyl records on a wall

Hooks, height, spacing and layouts, from the people who make records to hang.

What to hang it with

A 12-inch record weighs roughly 120 to 180 grams, so it needs very little. A small nail or a screw with a wall plug, or a removable adhesive hook rated well above that weight, will hold one. Every PastForward record comes with a wall hook in the box.

How high

Put the centre of the record, or the centre of a group of records, at roughly eye level: about 145 to 150 cm from the floor. Above a bed or sofa, leave about 20 to 25 cm between the furniture and the bottom of the lowest record.

How far apart

Records are about 30.5 cm across. Leave 5 to 8 cm between them so each one reads as its own piece. Use the same gap everywhere and the wall looks planned.

Three layouts that work

A straight row of three above a bed or desk. A grid of four or nine for a feature wall, measured out with paper circles taped up first. Or a loose diagonal climbing a staircase wall, keeping the gap between records the same.

Keeping them flat

Vinyl softens with heat. Keep records off walls that get direct afternoon sun or sit right next to a heater or kitchen stove, and the label print stays brighter too.

Questions

Can I hang a record without drilling?

Yes. A removable adhesive hook rated for a few hundred grams holds a single record on a smooth painted wall.

Will a hung record still play?

Ours are made to hang, not to play. The album art is set into the centre label.
