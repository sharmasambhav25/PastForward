## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Real 12-inch upcycled vinyl records with the album you love on the centre label, made to hang on your wall.
**What it does:** PastForward takes vinyl records that already had a life, cleans them up and sets album artwork into the centre label, so a real record becomes wall decor (wall hook in the box). ## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Walls that don't say anything about you, and decor options that are either cheap-looking or expensive.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Mass-market posters (Amazon/Meesho/Flipkart): thin paper, generic, everyone has the same one.
- Buying actual vinyl as decor: ₹2,500–5,000+ new, often not the albums you want available in India, and wasted if you have no turntable.
- Print-it-yourself: low quality, no frame/hook, looks DIY.
**What it costs them:** A room that feels temporary; a forgettable gift.
**Emotional tension:** Wanting to show taste without looking try-hard; worried online decor won't match the photos; gifting anxiety (will it arrive in time? will they like it?).

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Can I play it?" | No, it's made to hang. The grooves are real, the label is our artwork. Treat it as a wall piece. |
| "Is it a real record?" | Yes, a real 12-inch upcycled record. Show handling/close-up photos and video. |
| "Will it arrive in time for the gift?" | 7–10 days anywhere in India; publish clear gift cut-off dates per occasion. |
| "No COD?" | UPI, cards, net banking on a secure Shopify checkout. (COD not available yet.) |
| "What if it breaks in transit?" | Email within 7 days with a photo and we replace it. |
| "Small marks on the record?" | It's upcycled; surface marks are part of its history (stated in returns policy). Set this expectation before purchase. |

**Anti-persona:** Audiophiles/collectors wanting playable or original pressings; buyers needing COD; international buyers; anyone wanting a pristine factory-new surface.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Bare or generic walls; cheap posters that curl and fade; gift fatigue.
**Pull:** A real record with *their* album on it at ₹999; matching sets; custom.
**Habit:** Default to Amazon/Meesho posters; "I'll decorate later".
**Anxiety:** Product won't match photos; delivery timing; prepaid-only; legitimacy of a new Instagram brand.
